How the optimizer decides

Transparent rules, with you in control

01 · Fixed commitments

Available hours define the planning window; integrations can later supply meetings and commute blocks.

02 · Priority and deadlines

High-priority and soon-due tasks are placed earlier, without ignoring overdue work.

03 · Realistic duration

Tasks that don't fit are left unscheduled instead of overloading your day.

04 · Your approval

The generated plan is a proposal. It does not silently change external calendars.
